Note: Rosenbrock_2D's large gumbel_b/mean_bias values reflect its raw objective
scale (values in the thousands), not a qualitatively different failure mode --
KS_stat (scale-invariant) is the metric that determines PASS/WARN/FAIL.

Mechanism: the Gumbel formula assumes independence across roi_candidates
(product of per-candidate CDFs). With few real data points and an RBF kernel,
many of the 1000 broad-search candidates fall within one lengthscale of each
other and are highly correlated under the true GP posterior -- violating that
independence assumption and inflating the Gumbel-estimated max relative to the
true (correlated) empirical maximum (mean_bias is positive in all 15 rows).
