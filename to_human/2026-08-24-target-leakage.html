The freeze had a mechanism, and it was a bug

MF-DRO · Hartmann 6D · autoresearch progress · 24 Aug 2026

Every prior DRO number in this project is now suspect.

A target-leakage bug was found in the Decision Transformer readout — after the 1280-run inventory and all existing findings were generated. It affects both the multi-fidelity and single-fidelity paths. Nothing measured before commit 7bcc3b8 can be compared against a post-fix result without being re-run.

What the bug was

The DT sequence is four tokens per step — [rtg, btg, state, action] — with the action token at index 4t+3. The causal mask is triu(diagonal=1), which lets a position attend to itself. The readout took h[:, 3::4]: the action token, whose embedding encodes the true actions_x/actions_ell.

So the location head, the fidelity head and the score head were each predicting from their own target. Then at inference, propose_mf has no action to embed — the action is precisely what it is computing — so it zeroes that slot. The model's single most predictive input is present during training and absent at inference.

Why this resolves the standing question

The open question was whether the freeze had one mechanism or two — a fidelity-head threshold bug could explain MF-DRO, but never SF-DRO, which freezes 12/12 and has no fidelity head.

The SF path (get_action_hidden_states) carried the identical bug: [:, 2::3], also the action token, in its 3-token layout. One mechanism covers both — and it matches the observed ordering, where SF-DRO froze more than MF-DRO.

A second, unrelated mechanism also got separated out. MF-GP-UCB's 100% freeze is not the DRO pathology — it has no DT. It is cost-ratio degeneration to all-LF selection, already documented at mf_baselines.py:206 and confirmed by a smoke run (n_hf_queries = 0). The old "100% freeze" column was pooling two unrelated causes.

Running now in flight

The binding question is unanswered: does fixing the leak actually change end-to-end regret? No post-fix multi-seed run under the frozen protocol existed. One is running.

Still open

  • RTG still does not move the decision. Sweeping the RTG target 0.1×–10× leaves the argmax bit-stable. Score vectors do shift (corr 0.986–0.9999), so the pathway is connected but far too weak. A degenerate reward embedding has been ruled out.
  • The score head is close to a fixed acquisition function. argmax(score) matched argmax(mu_H) on 7/10 candidate pools — down from 100%, so partial independence exists, but corr is still 0.944.
  • The fidelity head drifts toward LF during training, and median p_pred sits below 0.5 even under Bernoulli sampling.
  • Early signal worth noting: MF-MI-Greedy's regret at matched cost=200 is spreading 0.19–1.54 across seeds, versus the inherited "0.279" figure — more evidence the old cross-method numbers were never cost-matched.

Confound to respect

All of the fixes above landed together before the RTG and score-head measurements were taken. The residual RTG-insensitivity therefore cannot yet be attributed to any single change. The ablation flags exist for exactly this and the next experiments should use them one at a time.
