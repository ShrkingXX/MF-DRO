MF-DRO 0.5047 ± 0.0395 · MI-Greedy 0.5091 ± 0.1266 · GP-UCB 1.7934 ± 0.1223, at matched cost ~200. PROTOCOL.md permits “no within-frame fix closes the gap”; that is the honest headline.

But the named pathology is resolved

0/10 runs frozen, vs a pre-fix 9/12 (75%). Mean regret 1.31 → 0.505 (only the post-fix number is trustworthy).

The cause: target leakage in the DT readout

4 tokens/step [rtg, btg, state, action]; the causal mask triu(diagonal=1) lets a position attend to itself; the readout took h[:, 3::4] — the action token, which embeds the true action. Every head predicted from its own target, and propose_mf must zero that slot at inference.

ablation: zeroing a_emb cost the fidelity head 15.6% of its loss pre-fix, 0.7% post-fix

The SF path carried the identical bug — decisive, because SF-DRO freezes 12/12 and has no fidelity head, so the competing explanation could never cover it.

So the conditioning pathway is inert, but the weights are not — and the weight-driven changes are close to regret-neutral (H6 n=30: +0.075, CI [−0.011, +0.161], p=0.0795). MF-DRO re-fits rather than conditions, and the re-fitting does real work that does not pay.

Where I corrected myself

  • “Continued training is inert.” Nearly claimed at n=7. H7 shows it changes 18% of decisions — they just buy nothing.
  • “Bit-identical proposals.” From a 4-record smoke; wrong at 377 records.
  • “Freezing helps 40%.” n=1. The estimate then changed sign twice (−0.208 → +0.075).
  • “Wrong-basin convergence explains regret.” True as description (7/10 seeds) but corr(d(x*), regret)=+0.09 — it predicts nothing.
  • “LF-drift destroys HF willingness.” 4/9 runs went the other way.
  • “Widen alpha_rtg to fix the RTG confound.” It is a no-op — the floor binds 0.0% of the time at 0.1. H9 voided itself on its own manipulation check.

Still open

Is RTG inert, or starved? The realised band is clamped to [0.5,1.0] (CV 0.232) by the batch-max normalisation — which my own RTG-cap fix introduced while correctly removing a different pathology. Separating the two needs a different conditioning quantity plus a retrain. A paper claiming “the DT ignores its return conditioning” would overstate the evidence until then.
